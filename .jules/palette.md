## 2025-01-24 - Accessible Language Toggles with Flags
**Learning:** Using purely decorative flag emojis for language selection buttons creates an accessibility barrier (screen readers may mispronounce or skip them). Furthermore, without explicitly wrapping such visually ambiguous icons with a descriptive Tooltip and standardizing them as accessible toggles via `aria-pressed`, both sighted users (who might not recognize a specific flag immediately) and screen-reader users suffer.
**Action:** When implementing icon-only or purely visual state-toggling buttons (like language/flag selections), explicitly combine a Tooltip (to aid sighted users on hover/focus) with `aria-label` (for the accessible name), `aria-pressed` (for toggle state), and `aria-hidden="true"` (to suppress unhelpful readouts of the decorative element itself).
