## 2026-04-19 - Accessible Accordions\n**Learning:** Custom div-based accordion headers often lack keyboard accessibility and screen reader support, requiring a specific set of ARIA attributes and event handlers to function properly.\n**Action:** When implementing custom accordions or clickable rows, always ensure they have `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` linked to the target's `id`, focus-visible styles, and an `onKeyDown` handler that supports 'Enter' and 'Space' (calling `e.preventDefault()` on Space).
